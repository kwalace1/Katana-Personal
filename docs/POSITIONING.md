# Katana Personal — Positioning

## One-liner

**Katana is your calm daily OS: one next step today, Ask that can act, Together only if you want it — private on this device.**

## The loop (always teach this)

1. **Today** — decide the one next step, capture what matters, close the day  
2. **Ask** — a day guide that already knows your plate and can draft small actions  
3. **Together** — optional friends, shared plans, Circles — only what you choose to share  

Plan / Life modules (tasks, habits, health, notes…) are **depth when you need them**, not the home story.

## Anti-positions (what we are not)

- Not Notion (wiki / second brain)  
- Not Todoist (endless task list)  
- Not a water / habit tracker with extras  
- Not “AI chat for life” as the product — Ask is a guide inside the day loop  

Water, habits, and health are **signals inside the day**, not the product.

## In-product voice

- Say **what** and **why** in one short line under section labels when the surface is easy to misread.  
- Prefer “daily OS / day loop” language over feature lists.  
- Privacy is a feature: “private on this device” / “only what you choose to share.”  

## Success check

After ~60 seconds, a cold user should say something closer to:

> “It’s a daily loop with a guide that can act, and friends if I want — not just a to-do list.”
